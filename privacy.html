Privacy Policy

Boriken Genetics Logbook · Effective October 23, 2026

The Logbook is an offline record-keeping app. It has no account and no sign-in, and we do not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no analytics, ads or tracking.

Your data

Everything you enter (plants, journal entries, patients, transaction records, photos) is stored only on your device.

iCloud sync and backup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud sync, your logbook is stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. If you turn on iCloud backup, a backup file is saved to your own iCloud Drive. We cannot access either. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add plant photos or videos to your logbook. They stay on your device (and in your own iCloud if you use sync).

Deleting your data

Delete data in the app (More › Backups & Export › Clear all data) or delete the app. To delete the iCloud copy, use More › Settings › Remove iCloud copy, or iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
