Family-tree visual fixes. Public fixture only.

(a) and (b) were already in the fan drawn by 721679f (7 Nov), before
the B-4 ribbon patch. The pre-B-4 screenshots in handoff/r19-tree/shots
show two Azulejo circles and two ribbons, and outer labels cut at the
rim. B-4 commit 8e6f09e only shrank that fan; it did not add the second
drawing.
(c) The title badge and the Lineage highlight are also from 721679f.
onLin() treats every family tree as the Lineage tab, including one
opened from Breeding. The B-4 diffs do not change mark().

Azulejo labels are fitted to the open width of their own wedge, inset
from the rim and the dashed edges. Words wrap. A role line is kept only
when that row fits at 9px or larger. A name that still cannot fit keeps
a whole-word form, and the full wording stays on the accessible name.

The public fixture is Fixture Child, mother Fixture Mother With A Long
Label, father Fixture Father With A Long Label, four Not recorded
grandparents, and one sibling.

  node /path/to/b12-tree-visual/b63-tree-visual-patch.js .
