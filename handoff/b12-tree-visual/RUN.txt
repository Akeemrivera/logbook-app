Family-tree visual fixes. Public fixture only.

(a) and (b) were already in the fan drawn by 721679f (7 Nov), before
the B-4 ribbon patch. The pre-B-4 screenshots in handoff/r19-tree/shots
show two Azulejo circles and two ribbons, and outer labels cut at the
rim. B-4 commit 8e6f09e only shrank that fan; it did not add the second
drawing.
(c) The title badge and the Lineage highlight are also from 721679f.
onLin() treats every family tree as the Lineage tab, including one
opened from Breeding. The B-4 diffs do not change mark().

The r2 label check only asked for a viewport box and no ellipsis, so
the middle-ring grandparents still collided. This revision places each
Azulejo fan label inside its own wedge. An empty slot draws
"+ Not" / "recorded". The "Mother of …" line stays on the accessible
name, and is drawn only when that row fits in the same block. The font
stays 10 user px.

The public fixture is a plant with mother Northern lights #5, father
Romulan, four Not recorded grandparents, and one sibling.
