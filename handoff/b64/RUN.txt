What changed
- Family tree: build57.js / build57.css are the approved b62-based tree
  (8 styles: walnut, ceiba, roots, azulejo, mobile, cert, river, window),
  straight lines only. build60.js / build60.css go back to the b62 files
  (no curved connectors). The b63 tree-layout rules in build63.css are
  removed; its non-tree rules (inspection table, 248px sidebar, Plants
  header fit, sidebar theme-match) are unchanged.
- B-11: Mothers & Fathers clicks are delegated on the document, so
  "+ Add mother" works after mfpOpen() moves the list (build13.js). The
  QA UI test dismisses the keyboard, scrolls like a user, and opens the
  Mothers & Fathers page before "+ Add mother" (LogbookQAUITests.swift).
  The long-name test value is now a generic name of the same length.
- B-13: production plants do not get their own tree. A clone opens its
  linked mother's tree; seed-started plants keep their own tree; crosses
  and projects link to their mother's and father's trees. Optional
  "Clone of" picker on a production clone. One approved, backed-up,
  idempotent migration links one production clone to its mother
  (record ids are matched by one-way hash, not stored). Siblings exclude
  self and dedupe by id.
- B-15: each plant keeps its own saved style (1-8), and the 8 styles are
  structurally different (b62 layouts restored).
- B-16: Archive on a mother/father profile is Retire, with a confirm and
  Restore. Retired plants move to "Retired / Archived" and stay in trees.
  The round title badge is aligned to the first line of the name
  (build41.js, build41-ui.css, build42.js, mobile.js, build13.js).
- Version: APP_BUILD 64, CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION 64 (CFBundleVersion),
  MARKETING_VERSION 1.0. Service-worker cache v108 -> v109.
